Shloka of the Day

A moment for devotion.

ॐ
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय
सूर्यकोटि समप्रभः
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव
शुभ कार्येषु सर्वदा॥

O Lord with the curved trunk and immense form, whose brilliance equals millions of suns, please make all my auspicious works free from obstacles, always.
